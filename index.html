                                                      Backup

                                                      Everything you log lives in this browser's storage. Sync keeps your devices in step, but it is not a backup — it makes them agree, so a bad write or a wipe agrees everywhere too. Download a copy you keep yourself. Your API keys and your sign-in are deliberately left out, so the file is safe to mail to yourself or leave in iCloud.
